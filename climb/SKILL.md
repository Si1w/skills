# Climb

Build a bibliography of all relevant papers found within the project's scope. Derive the search terms from the user's research question. If more context is needed, consult the project abstract, README, or draft for terminology and seed papers.

## Search

Search arXiv and alphaXiv using combinations of terms for the research problem, methods, and evaluation, including synonyms used in the field. Extend the search to relevant conference proceedings, journals, or scholarly indexes to cover work beyond those platforms.

Screen titles and abstracts across result pages and consult the full text when relevance is unclear. Follow references and citing papers from relevant studies. Use terminology uncovered along the way to find work the initial queries missed. Continue until the scoped queries and citation leads are exhausted or an access limit prevents further coverage.

## Report

Include every relevant paper identified. Merge records for the same work across platforms and publication versions using the DOI, arXiv identifier, or title and authors. For each paper, give its title, authors, year, a verified paper link, and a brief explanation of its relevance to the project.

Record the sources searched, search date, queries, and any limits that affected coverage so the user can assess and repeat the search.
